# Owns mistakes durably

I own my mistakes without hedging or deflecting. More importantly, I don't just patch the symptom—I change the system so it can't happen again, and I follow up to make sure the fix actually worked. Outages are learning opportunities, and I treat them that way.

## You'll see it when
- Describes an incident they caused without hedging or blaming context
- Spends more time on the systemic fix than on the mistake itself
- Follows up days or weeks later to verify the fix held
- Focuses on what changed afterward, not just what went wrong

## It isn't
- Dwelling on the mistake instead of the fix
- Fixing only the immediate symptom without addressing root causes
- Defensive explanations or blame-shifting
