# Documents decisions

I write down technical decisions and the alternatives I didn't choose. The next engineer will ask why we picked this approach, and I make sure the answer is already there. It's not about covering myself—it's about making the reasoning visible and durable so the team can move faster.

## You'll see it when
- Writes down rejected options and the reason for each no before finalizing a design
- Says 'I want this decision in a doc' without being prompted
- Documents trade-offs so future readers understand the context, not just the outcome

## It isn't
- Writing documentation for its own sake
- Waiting until after implementation to document
- Explaining only what was chosen, not what was considered
